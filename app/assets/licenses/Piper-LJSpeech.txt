English voice: Piper en_US-ljspeech-medium, trained from scratch by Bryce Beattie on the LJ Speech Dataset.
Recordings by Linda Johnson for LibriVox, annotation by Keith Ito: public domain.
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/

The app runs the voice with native/vits.cpp, with its weights reduced to 8 and 12 bits.
Bryce Beattie publishes the voice as public domain: https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
