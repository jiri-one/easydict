EasyDict is an offline English–Czech and Czech–English dictionary. It is the first open-source translator that is completely open, dictionary data included: more than 150,000 translations of the free dictionary of the Svobodné slovníky project, formerly the GNU/FDL English-Czech Dictionary.

<ul>
<li>Offline Czech and English spoken pronunciation</li>
<li>Results while you type, without an internet connection</li>
<li>Search by the start of a word, by whole words or anywhere in the text</li>
<li>Czech words are also found in other forms: "psa" finds "pes"</li>
<li>Diacritics and capital letters do not matter</li>
<li>Czech and English user interface, light and dark theme</li>
</ul>

The dictionary is maintained in the EasyDict repository and anybody can improve it.
