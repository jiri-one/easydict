Welcome to EasyDict

The first open-source translator that is completely open, dictionary data included.

EasyDict is an English–Czech and Czech–English dictionary with more than 150,000 translations. All of it is open: the apps and the website are free software under the GNU GPL 3, and anyone may use and change the dictionary data under the GNU FDL. The dictionary continues the Svobodné slovníky project, and it also finds Czech words typed in other forms: “psa” finds “pes” (dog), “očima” finds “oko” (eye).

Where to find EasyDict
  • Here on the website: the search is on the right (at the very bottom on a phone).
  • The web app: EasyDict in the browser, on a computer or a phone. It searches the same dictionary as this page.
  • Apps for Linux, Windows, macOS and Android: they work offline too. On Android, select a word in any app and choose “Look up in EasyDict”; on a computer, EasyDict can also look up text copied to the clipboard. The first release is on its way, on GitHub, Flathub and F-Droid.
History

EasyDict started in 2020 as a desktop app in Python and GTK 3, which was also on Flathub. A version for GTK 4 and libadwaita followed in 2023–2024. Both live on in the archived easydict-gtk repository. The website went from CherryPy to Django. In 2026 the whole project moved into one repository, jiri-one/easydict. The dictionary data have a new home there, since the svobodneslovniky.cz website is gone, and the app was rewritten in Flutter to run on phones too.

What comes next

A simple way for users to suggest new translations and corrections. Volunteers will approve the suggestions (for now, my friend Alex from England and I can do it; Alex practises Czech that way and makes sure the English is right), and approved translations will show up on the website and in the web app right away. Later, more dictionaries and, hopefully, a community around them.
